iPhone Duo: Apple's first foldable, now in Armenia

A 5.4-inch iPhone that opens into a 7.6-inch screen. What it does well, what it gives up, and what it costs in Yerevan.

What it is

Closed, it is a compact 5.4-inch phone. Open, it becomes a 7.6-inch screen, about 50% larger than the iPhone 18 Pro Max display. The frame and hinge are titanium, it is rated IP68 against dust and water, and it weighs 254 g: 11.3 mm thick folded, 5.2 mm open.

Screens

Both screens are OLED with ProMotion up to 120 Hz, Always-On and up to 3,000 nits outdoors. The inner screen has a nano-texture surface against reflections. iOS 27 puts two apps side by side on the big screen, and StandBy works on either screen without a charger.

Chip and battery

The A20 Pro (2 nm) with a vapor chamber, as in the iPhone 18 Pro. There are two batteries, one in each half. Apple quotes up to 24 hours of typical use, up to 44 hours of video on the outer screen and up to 31 hours on the inner one. A 60 W USB-C charger fills it to 50% in about 20 minutes; MagSafe charges at up to 25 W.

Cameras

  • 48 MP main camera with a 2× zoom crop, and a 48 MP ultra wide that also shoots macro.
  • A 12 MP Center Stage camera on the outside, and a camera hidden under the inner screen for video calls.
  • The rear cameras work as selfie cameras too: fold it and use the outer screen as the viewfinder.

What it gives up

  • No Face ID. Touch ID is in the side button.
  • eSIM only, with no SIM tray. Ask your operator for an eSIM before you buy.
  • No 4× telephoto like the iPhone 18 Pro, only the 2× crop.

Price in Armenia

In the US it starts at $1,999 for 256 GB and goes up to $3,199 for 2 TB. In Yerevan on 18 October 2026, REDstore has the 256 GB at 1 399 000 ֏ and the 512 GB at 1 499 000 ֏, both colours in stock. No shop lists the 1 TB or 2 TB yet. Prices change daily; the product page always shows the current ones.

Who it is for

For people who read, watch and work on the phone: documents, two apps at once, video on a near-tablet screen that still fits in a pocket. If the camera matters most, or the price, the iPhone 18 Pro Max is the better buy: from 639 000 ֏ in Armenia.
